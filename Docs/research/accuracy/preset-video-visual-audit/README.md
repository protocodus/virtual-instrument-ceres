# Primary video inspection: presets and amplifier

Three creator videos were inspected on YouTube through paused, seeked playback. The local evidence contains **82 source screenshots**, 80 recorded seek observations, and contact sheets. This is a bounded still-frame inspection, not continuous viewing, audio analysis, a gate transcription, or proof that no useful passage exists between the inspected times. No source has yet been promoted to a controlled numerical benchmark.

The [capture manifest](report.json) binds every source screenshot, contact sheet and exact browser observation. The screenshots are local, git-ignored research evidence; public playback does not establish redistribution permission. The two initial AutomaticGainsay frames at65/120s precede the retained structured seek collection; their image hashes are retained, but full tool-result logs for those two seeks are not claimed. Every later source screenshot has a recorded target and returned video time. Source frames were inspected in contact sheets, with individual enlargements for the passages discussed above.

The first Doctor Mix capture was an advertisement. Its duration disagreed with the source metadata and visual content; it is excluded from all source contact sheets and counts. Later captures check `ad-showing=false`, original video ID, decoded duration and paused state. The manifest also records a denied screenshot destination, an ambiguous dialog selector, an empty timed-text response, loading transcript panels and an intercepted pre-roll click. None was silently substituted with source evidence. Standard visible cookie rejection, quality and advertisement-skip controls were used; no access restriction was bypassed and no audio/video was downloaded.

This inspection improves source qualification; it does not improve a recording loss or justify a DSP/default change. In particular, physical slider positions are not measured chip control voltages. The separate [calibration rank audit](../calibration-identifiability/README.md) explains why unknown BR, touch and EG scale can confound a shared cutoff fit. The earlier [source search](../public-reference-search-03/README.md) remains a metadata-only historical result; its previously unaudited preset leads have now received the limited visual inspection described here.

A subsequent [Part2 filter inspection](../filter-video-inspection/README.md) now records34 additional frames and the buffered manual-cutoff source path. It finds a promising initial zero-EG setup but does not yet establish the two visible, fixed-Q plateaus required for the proposed relative-response measurement. The [Sub-Oscillator episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AXZ8ipkXZEw) remains an unqualified component lead. Public sources are not asserted to be exhausted.
