# Oscillator one-shot and conditioned WSC input

This is an experimental reduced circuit correction. It replaces the raw recharge triangle at the exposed saw output with a conditioned waveform, and passes that same reset shape through the existing ideal fold/sine transfer. It does not recover the hidden Yamaha waveshaper transistor circuit.

The primary [CS Series Servicing Guide](https://rcarchives.com/m/YamahaCSSeriesSynthesizerServicingGuidePOSTED.pdf), printed28-31, separates the raw capacitor, monostable and output waveshaper. Charge balance gives `f = Kv/(RFT*Iref*tau)`. The Normal frequency table gives523.2Hz/V. The [CS-50 M-board schematic](https://bama.edebris.com/download/yamaha/cs-50/Yamaha%20CS-50%20Service%20Manual%202%20of%202%20%28%2011%20x%2017%20-%20Double%20Sided%20%29.pdf), PDF12/printed16, and guide printed30 identify the Normal40k plus1k-trim branch; nominal Iref is800uA. Thus default reset is now `1/(523.2*40000*.0008)=59.728593us`. Across40-41k it is58.27-59.73us under the nominal current law. This is not the26.4-32.4us bare22k..27k times1200pF RC product, and the120pF compensation capacitor is not the timing cap. Current/trim tolerances remain unknown. The existing engineering setter now permits0.1-60us so it can represent the derived default; existing keys and ABI fields are retained.

With phase zero at reset onset, r=f*tau and fixed current normalization, the descending capacitor segment is `p=1-2*phase`. The published OUT7 sketch shows a brief high segment and step rather than the raw recharge slope. The chosen reduction uses `p=1` before r, then `p=1-2*phase`. **A perfectly flat clamp and its exact2r step are assumptions consistent with the sketch, not measured specifications.** WSC inverts p for audio saw. Its existing ideal triangle-fold/sine transfer now consumes p, giving a plateau1 followed by cos(2pi*phase), instead of bypassing the reset with a separate ideal phase ramp. Slice trim and internal reset compensation remain unidentified; upper-note sine changes are particularly conditional.

The r=f*tau phase model is a constant-frequency reduction, not a clocked physical monostable under rapidly changing frequency or reset controls. Voltage and slope discontinuities receive analytic BLEP/BLAMP corrections, including the sine step. No wavetable or sample playback is used. The pulse comparator retains its independently documented fixed rising edge and moving falling edge. At the normal50-90% pulse widths, the existing r<=.35 guard keeps the plateau from swallowing the falling edge. Deep PWM can reduce duty below r even at ordinary upper notes; there the independently documented fixed-edge rule remains a WSC approximation, not a literal comparator of p. Above approximately5.86kHz at nominal reset, that guard becomes a defensive software extrapolation; it does not reproduce a physical fixed59.7us one-shot beyond that point. The existing frequency/sample-rate guard also remains.

`Tests/OscillatorCircuitTests.cpp` independently integrates both piecewise waveforms, applies the known triangular BLEP edge transfer, and sums Fourier aliases at n+kN to predict sampled coefficients. It checks16 harmonics, DC, current-derived slope, shared reset, pulse invariance, nominal calibration and extreme-rate bounds. Direct waveform relationships, pitch, embedded allocation and native/embedded audio are also covered by the existing contracts. These establish the chosen reduction and implementation, not a calibrated hardware waveform or an alias-free oscillator.

A previous candidate was already executed in `/tmp/ceres-cs50-1x8LGd/experiments/vco-conditioning-01`. Its frozen controls showed that a raw59us triangle strongly worsened magnitude matching; conditioned59us recovered most of that loss but did not beat the6us baseline. Late-E4 H2-H32 MAE was0.11447/2.18828/0.19846dB for old6/raw59/conditioned59; late-D4 .81179/1.97016/.82292; dry saw .33340/.66626/.34632. All square WAVs were exact. These adverse/neutral results are retained. That earlier candidate did not apply the present same-ramp sine fold and is neither an independent holdout nor proof of this default. No recording, gain, phase, gate or filter parameter was reoptimized here.
