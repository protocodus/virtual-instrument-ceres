# Manual low-pass response: positive source path, incomplete recording test

The [creator's filter episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ivVAJ13Tzvc) contains a promising unmodulated setup. The inspected60s close-up shows VCF IL/AL at zero, HP cutoff low, LP cutoff high and both resonance controls low. The90s view shows playing with LP still high. This separates the initial manual-filter demonstration from the later envelope and global-control demonstrations.

The [schematic review](source-review.md) confirms a useful distinction: PN1's B10k manual cutoff slider drives a PRA unity follower before the T71 diode/bus. Its passive wiper resistance must not be substituted for the output impedance of that buffered path. The neighboring factory-preset voltage dividers are a different source. Under fixed other inputs and an approximately constant conducting-diode drop, the provisional LP law predicts6.5octaves of variable-gm change per full slider travel. This relative prediction can be tested without identifying the absolute BR/trim offset. It remains conditional on loading, actual diode behavior and constant voice/source state.

The closer90–146s inspection **does not yet supply two visible, stationary cutoff positions at unchanged Q and voice state**. The cutoff cap is covered during the initial sweep at98/102s; it is back high at106s. Resonance changes in the110–118s region. The following sweep at120/122s is partly obscured by the performer, and126s has a different resonant setting. These are sparse still observations, not a continuous gesture reconstruction. They rule out treating these particular snapshots as the proposed simple two-state calibration; they do not prove the whole video has no usable data.
