# Ceres: hardware realism research (2026-10-20)

Ceres models the Yamaha CS-50. This pass read the primary sources again,
looking for hardware behaviour the engine still omits or simplifies. The engine
already carries a deep circuit model (VCO conditioning, IG00156 filters,
capacitor envelopes, BRILLIANCE/RESONANCE buses, PRA ring modulator, SUB
routing, Sustain I/II), so these items target what is left.

## Sources

- **Guide**: Yamaha CS Series Synthesizer Servicing Guide,
  <https://rcarchives.com/m/YamahaCSSeriesSynthesizerServicingGuidePOSTED.pdf>.
  Page numbers are printed page / PDF page.
- **SM1, SM2**: Yamaha CS-50 Service Manual 1 of 2 and 2 of 2,
  <https://bama.edebris.com/manuals/yamaha/cs-50/>.
- File:line references are to `Source/DSP/ceres.cpp` at `35c1aaf`, before the change. Items 1–5 shipped in 0.3.0.

## Findings

| 1 | **Portamento and glissando.** Panel 3 has a PORTAMENTO/GLISSANDO switch and a time lever. ## Already faithful (checked, no change)

- PWM: the PW lever spans 50–90 %, PWM gives ±40 %, and duty saturates near 99 % (Guide p.31). These match `pulse_width`, `pwm_depth` .4 and the `.01..0.99` clamp.
- PITCH: the ladder reference runs 3–6 V around 4 V, which is −498/+702 cents. This matches `tune_cents` −500..+700.
- The ring trigger fires on "any KEY-ON signal" (Guide p.19). This matches the physical-key ledger.
- Power-on key code is C4 in every channel (Guide p.17). Board reset uses note 60.

### Checked and not changed

- **Sustain control Vbe.** The SUS/release minimum selector ends in an emitter follower, so the panel release CV reaches the EG about 0.6 V low when SUS is not lower (Guide p.61 / PDF 70; SM2 p.8). Not implemented: the service spec gives the same 10 ms–10 s endpoints for D and R levers, so whether the drop is absorbed by the published law or trimmed out cannot be separated.
- **Portamento time constants.** The guide's table maps PS lever voltage to comparators a/b/c: −6.5…−1.72 V large (3.3M only), −1.72…3.81 V medium (470k gate), 3.81…7.14 V very small (direct gate), 7.14…8.5 V counter off (Guide p.25 / PDF 34). Not implemented: the clock oscillator (IC24, BA617) law that relates the lever to `glide_rate` is not printed, and the guide (1 µF) and schematic (0.033 µF) disagree on the capacitor.
- **LC control capacitor.** IC5's LI pin has 0.01 µF to ground (SM2 p.16). With R7's 53.5k source it would be a 0.54 ms smoother on tremolo and LEVEL, but the R7 sheet rates every mixing node into a 1 kΩ load, which makes it about 10 µs. Not implemented.
- **Key coder priority.** Implemented in the third pass (row 19).
- **Expression pedal.** Implemented in the fourth pass (row 22).
- **Already faithful:** PW 680k and PWM 100k sum into the WSC at equal full-scale currents, matching ±40 % (SM2 p.16); VCO II is exponential at 0.2/6.7/222 Hz (Guide p.58); the SUB square's 15k/1.8k divider meets the sine's 1.5 V peak; the pulse's leading edge is fixed and only the fall moves (Guide p.31).

### Checked and not changed (third pass)

- **Portamento clock and integrator.** Implemented in the fourth pass (row 20).
- **Vibrato depth.** The VCO VIB input is exponential at 50 cents per 1.5 V (guide p.27); R7 feeds it V1/V2 through 100k per board. `vibrato_cents` is already in engineering units, so the lever taper belongs to an adapter.
- **Oscillator pitch.** A probe reads 0.00 cents from equal temperament through C7 at the top octave, matching the guide's ±1 cent tuning at C3 and C7.
- **Expression with no pedal.** −15 V reaches EI through 27k (TP1 −3.83 V) and the PS88 LED draws about 4.7 mA, the fully lit end of row 22's curve, which that row bypasses exactly.
- **Panel lever loading.** Implemented for the two linear levers in the fourth pass (row 23). The earlier note's 29 kΩ for TVR5's load was wrong; see that row.
- **Tone-preset boards (T71/T72).** Implemented in the fourth pass (row 21).
- **R7 fL test value.** Guide p.59 gives fL 0.64 V into 1 kΩ with every input at +10 V; 39k/220k/47k×3 predicts about 0.86 V. fH (0.45 V) agrees. Unresolved; recorded only.
- **SUB square polarity.** IC10-2 makes the square as −sign(sine), inverted against the engine's convention. The SUB clock is free-running, so this is inaudible.
- **Ring modulator.** The carrier is the IG00150's sine (pin 6, through 100 µF and 33k; pin 7's sawtooth only reaches TP2), as modelled. The signal enters pin 1 through 330k against 470 Ω to the AC-grounded bias node, about 1/700 of the bus, far inside the 470 Ω-degenerated pair's linear range, so the signal side is linear. VR5 trims carrier leakage below 3 mVp-p (guide p.62), consistent with none. *Superseded 2026-10-20 by [ring-authenticity-2026-10-20.md](ring-authenticity-2026-10-20.md): the 33k is in series with the 100 µF, so the carrier at pins 7–8 is a 4.7 mV sine and the multiplier is linear; the signal resistor is 330 Ω (guide p.52, overall diagram), not 330k.*
- **Envelope attack.** Gate 1 applies about 10 V (+15 V through 30k/60k) and the sequencer switches to decay at VA = VB (guide p.43): an RC aimed at the attack level, as the 1 % headroom model has it. The VCF-EG's SL pin is grounded on the M board, so its decay ends at 0 V, as modelled.
- **KAS sample-hold.** Each channel samples the D/A line through 2.2k and a TC4016 into 0.033 µF; the strobed time constant is below a millisecond, so there is no audible slew. The buffer's 560 Ω and 0.01 µF add 5.6 µs.
- **WSC sine.** The IG00158 rounds its triangle into a sine with an undocumented transfer, so the ideal sine stays.
